General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM) Program

The GNM program is designed to provide students with comprehensive clinical training, practical experience, and academic knowledge necessary to become competent nurses. The curriculum includes subjects like anatomy, physiology, pharmacology, midwifery, community health, and nursing ethics. Graduates are prepared to work in hospitals, clinics, and community health centers, serving diverse populations with compassion and skill.

  • Comprehensive clinical and academic training
  • Hands-on practical experience in healthcare settings
  • Focus on ethical and professional development
  • Opportunities for career growth and specializations
